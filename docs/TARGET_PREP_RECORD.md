# IL-4Rα target preparation — judgement calls and provenance

Generated by `scripts/01_target_prep.py`, `02_epitopes.py`, `03_crop_hlt.py`
(repo: `~/tnf-track3/bd-il4ra-pipeline`). Written 2026-11-21.

`TARGET_PREP.md` §7 requires this file to record **every judgement call** made at this
stage, because Round 1 may require us to explain our methodology by e-mail.

---

## 1. Which structure, and why

**1IAR chain B** is the design target. 2.30 Å, binary IL-4 / IL-4Rα complex, and its
SEQRES chain B is exactly **207** residues — identical to the UniProt P24394-1
extracellular topological domain (AA 26–232) the brief specifies.

**3BPN chain B** is used as an independent cross-check only (3.02 Å ternary complex).
Global Cα RMSD between the two over 188 shared residues is **1.10 Å**, so no selected
epitope sits in a region where the two crystal forms disagree.

Structures in complex with a known antibody (6WGL dupilumab, 6OEL, 8K4Q, 8Z8L) were
**deliberately not used as coordinate sources**, to keep the novelty provenance clean.
They appear only in the frozen novelty reference set.

## 2. Numbering — derived, not assumed

**`uniprot_resnum = pdb_resnum + 25`**

Derived from `SEQADV` records and asserted to be a single consistent offset, in **two
structures independently**:

| source | evidence |
|---|---|
| 1IAR | pdb 1 → UniProt 26 (MET→PHE), pdb 182 → UniProt 207 (CYS→ALA) |
| 3BPN | pdb 28/73/109/151 → UniProt 53/98/134/176 |

`derive_offset()` refuses to run if the records imply more than one offset. Separately,
all 188 observed residues were asserted to match SEQRES at their index — the off-by-one
detector. Written to `numbering_map.json` as a 207-row file.

**Hotspots are quoted in 1IAR chain B PDB numbering with a `T` prefix** (e.g. `T67`),
which the crops preserve. `verify_target()` re-parses each written crop and asserts the
amino-acid identity of every hotspot before any run.

## 3. Glycan mask — 10.0 Å, relaxed from 12.0 Å

**This is the most consequential judgement call on this page.**

All six N-glycosylation sequons (N53, N98, N128, N134, N176, N209) lie inside the
207-residue ECD, all six were verified as genuine `N-X-[ST]` with X≠P, and the assay
antigen is a mammalian-expressed Fc fusion — so all six are assumed occupied.

`TARGET_PREP.md` §2 sets 12.0 Å as a conservative default, relaxable to 10.0 Å **only if
masking leaves no viable epitope**. That condition was met:

| mask | best Fv-sized sub-site in E1 | hydrophobic | area |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12.0 Å | L42+L43 only | **2** | **302 Å²** |
| 10.0 Å | Y13,D67,V68,V69,S70,D125,N126,Y127,N130,H131 | **4** | **675 Å²** |

The documented minimums are >~3 hydrophobic residues (RFantibody README) and ≥600 Å²
burialable (`TARGET_PREP.md` §6). **At 12.0 Å nothing clears either.** At 12.0 Å the mask
removed 97 of 188 observed residues (52%); at 10.0 Å it removes 63 (34%).

Two independent supports:
- **IL-4 itself binds this surface despite the glycans** — its nearest heavy atom is
  9.0 Å from sequon N98's Cβ and 7.8 Å from N209's. A sub-nanomolar native ligand
  engages well inside the 12 Å shell.
- 3BPN's crystallographers mutated **N53Q, N98Q, N134Q, N176Q** to crystallise, which is
  independent evidence those sites are genuinely glycosylated — and the reason we do not
  simply trust a crystal structure's lack of modelled glycan.

Applied **uniformly**, not cherry-picked for E1. Both runs preserved under
`radius_study/`. Reverse with `GLYCAN_MASK_RADIUS=12.0 python scripts/01_target_prep.py`.

**Residual risk, stated plainly:** sequon N98 sits inside the IL-4 competitive site. If
that glycan is bulky, E1 designs may bind the model and not the antigen. This is the
largest silent-failure risk in the campaign, and it is why E2 and E3 exist on
independent surface.

## 4. Other exclusions

- **Membrane-proximal**: UniProt ≥ 218 (the last 15 of the ECD) excluded — occluded on
  the receptor and adjacent to the Fc-fusion junction.
- **Free cysteine**: 1IAR carries **C207A** (pdb 182). The real antigen therefore has
  **Cys** where our model has **Ala**. pdb 182/183 were in the raw IL-4 contact set and
  are excluded by the glycan mask anyway (5.6–7.5 Å from sequon N209), so no interface is
  built against a position whose identity differs from the real antigen.
- **Correction to the handoff docs**: 1IAR chain B has **three** disulfides —
  UniProt (34,44), **(54,84)**, (74,86). The docs list only two. All six Cys are paired;
  there is no unpaired Cys in the crystallised construct.

## 5. Epitopes

| arm | slots | n | hotspots (UniProt) | area | hydrophobic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** IL-4/IL-13 competitive site | 12 | 13 | D92, V94, D150, Y152 | 666 Å² | 4 |
| **E2** seed L109 | 5 | 11 | L61, W104, L109, L110 | 882 Å² | 5 |
| **E3** seed L139 | 3 | 10 | L140, R185, A187, A188 | 970 Å² | 6 |

E1 is computed, not hardcoded: 1IAR chain B residues with any heavy atom within 5.0 Å of
any IL-4 (chain A) heavy atom, minus both masks.

E2/E3 come from scoring all surface patches after masking — 73 scored, 43 passed the hard
gates, full table including every rejected patch and its rejection reason in
`patch_scoring.tsv`. Weights are **[ASSUMPTION] ours**: 0.30 area, 0.30 hydrophobic
fraction, 0.15 B-factor, 0.15 1IAR/3BPN agreement, 0.10 convexity. E2 and E3 centroids
are ≥14 Å apart and both ≥25 Å from E1, so the three arms are genuinely independent
surfaces rather than three views of one.

**Honest note:** E2 and E3 are *better design targets than E1* on every structural
criterion. E1 retains the largest slot allocation because it is the functionally
meaningful site (blocking IL-4/IL-13 signalling) and carries the strongest Round 2
narrative. If the E1 arm underperforms on real metrics, the split should move.

### Hotspot selection, and a bug it caught

Hotspots are scored `0.40*exposure + 0.35*centrality + 0.25*hydrophobic` **[ASSUMPTION]**,
restricted to the best Fv-sized sub-site. The first run selected **Y38, whose SASA is
7.6 Å² — a buried core residue**: centrality and hydrophobicity outvoted near-zero
exposure. A buried hotspot steers RFdiffusion at a surface that does not exist, and the
symptom (per the RFantibody README) is undocked antibodies, easily misread as a bad
epitope. Fixed with a hard **30 Å² exposure floor applied before ranking**, plus an
assertion on every returned hotspot. E1's hotspot changed Y38 → D92.

### Literature cross-check, resolved

`TARGET_PREP.md` §6 cites a third-party patent epitope at **L39, F41, L42, L43, D72,
Y74** with "numbering convention unverified". Tested against the actual sequence:
**6/6 residue identities match PDB (mature-protein) numbering; 0/6 match UniProt.** The
patent uses mature numbering, and five of its six residues are in our raw IL-4 contact
set. Used **only** as evidence that the region is antibody-accessible. No sequence from
that patent is used anywhere.

while E2 does not, crop fragmentation is the first thing to suspect, ahead of hotspots.
